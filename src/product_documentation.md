## Heavy-Duty Mobile Generators: Product & Industry Documentation

### 1. Industry Overview: Off-Grid Industrial Power
The mobile industrial generator industry provides critical infrastructure for outdoor operations requiring reliable, off-grid power for field teams. Modern industrial operations are moving away from smaller, less efficient portable batteries that frequently die mid-shift and cause operational delays. Instead, the industry standard focuses on solutions that are highly mobile to easily move between different site locations while delivering robust performance.

### 3. Operational Capabilities
Generators operating in this industrial class must meet stringent durability and output requirements:
* Units must provide consistent, high-output power capable of running industrial tools (such as drills and saws) alongside lighting rigs simultaneously.
* Equipment must be ruggedized for outdoor use across varying weather conditions.
* Fuel efficiency and ease of maintenance remain key priorities for field operations teams.

### 4. Standard Warranty & Maintenance Terms
Standard industry procurement agreements generally separate core equipment warranties from ongoing consumable costs:
* **Coverage:** Equipment typically includes a one (1) year standard warranty covering both parts and labor.
* **Routine Servicing:** Routine maintenance, such as oil changes and filter replacements, falls outside the standard warranty scope and is the responsibility of the client.
* **Consumables:** Ongoing fuel costs are not included in baseline equipment purchase agreements.
